8. How Whyfull works with your files

You should understand these facts before using Whyfull:

  1. Whyfull reads your disk. It scans folders you choose, or default locations, and reports sizes, file paths and cleanup suggestions. Reports and caches are stored on your computer.
  2. Whyfull never deletes anything automatically. Scanning and reporting do not change your files. Cleanup happens only when you explicitly ask for it.
  3. Cleanup is your action. When you choose to move items to the Trash, run a cleanup command recommended for the tool that owns the data (for example a package manager, container tool or model runner), or follow any other suggestion, you are the one initiating that action. Labels such as "safe", "rebuildable" or "cache" are best-effort guidance based on general knowledge of how other software behaves; they may be wrong for your setup, and other software can change without notice.
  4. Trash is not a backup. Items moved to the Trash may be removed permanently by your operating system, by you, or by other software, and owning-tool cleanup commands may delete data permanently. Some deleted data (for example downloaded AI models or caches) may be large or slow to restore, and some (for example uncommitted work in a git worktree) may not be restorable at all.
  5. You are responsible for your data. You are responsible for reviewing what you clean up, for keeping current backups of anything you care about, and for any loss of data, time, work or configuration that results from cleanup you initiate.
  6. Reports may contain private information. File paths and project names can be sensitive. You decide whether to share a report, for example with an AI agent or another person, and you are responsible for keeping reports, caches and the local viewer away from people you do not want to see them.
  7. Commands and AI agents are yours to check. Whyfull may show cleanup commands, or produce reports that you give to an AI coding agent, a script or another tool. Whether to run a command, and what an AI agent or other tool does with a Whyfull report, are your decisions and your responsibility. Read every command, and check every file or folder, before you run, remove or let anything else remove it.
  8. You use Whyfull at your own risk. You assume all risks of using Whyfull and of acting on its results, including the risk that a size, estimate, owner or label is wrong, that a cleanup removes something you needed, or that another program behaves differently than Whyfull expects.

3. The website and purchases

Payments: Stripe as merchant of record

Licenses are sold through Stripe Managed Payments. Stripe is the merchant of record for your purchase: it collects your payment, calculates and remits sales tax or VAT, issues receipts and invoices, and handles refunds and disputes. For that, Stripe collects and uses your payment details, billing address, tax location and related information as an independent controller, under its own privacy policy (https://stripe.com/privacy). Card numbers and other payment details are entered directly with Stripe and are never received or stored by us.

What we receive and store

After a purchase, Stripe sends us, and we store:

  • your email address (to send and recover your license key);
  • your name, if you provide it (it can be included in the license key);
  • the license id, the license key and the issue date;
  • Stripe order ids (the checkout session, customer and payment ids), and the amount and currency paid, so we can match refunds and answer support requests;
  • whether and when the license email was sent, and whether and when the purchase was refunded.

When you use the license recovery page, we use the email you enter to look up licenses and send a recovery email to that address.

To limit abuse of the license and recovery pages, we keep short-lived counters keyed by a one-way hash (SHA-256) of your IP address and, for the recovery page, of the email you enter. The raw IP address and email are not stored in these counters.

When you visit whyfull.app, our hosting provider processes standard technical data (such as IP address, user agent and requested URL) to deliver and protect the site. We do not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies on whyfull.app.

The /activate page reads the license key from the part of the link after #, which browsers do not send to the server, so the key is not logged by the website.

Why we use it

To deliver what you bought (issuing, sending and recovering license keys), to match refunds, to prevent fraud and abuse, and to answer support requests. In the European Union and the United Kingdom, the legal bases are the performance of our contract with you (issuing and recovering licenses), our legitimate interests (preventing abuse, answering support) and compliance with legal obligations.
