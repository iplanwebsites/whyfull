Your disk. Your decisions.

Does Whyfull delete anything?

Scanning doesn’t delete anything. Cleanup is a separate action you control; the optional viewer Trash feature requires confirmation.

Is every gigabyte reclaimable?

No. Some files matter; some caches cost time to rebuild. Whyfull helps you decide what to keep.

Where does the report go?

Whyfull runs locally. You choose what to share with your agent; reports can include private paths.

Go further. Open any folder.

The app and CLI include the same local disk viewer. Drill into folders, compare their sizes, and find what’s worth clearing out.

Or open the viewer from your terminal:

npx whyfull --view
