Your disk. Your decisions.

Does Whyfull delete anything?

Scanning doesn’t delete anything. Cleanup is a separate action you control; the optional viewer Trash feature requires confirmation.

Is every gigabyte reclaimable?

No. Some files matter; some caches cost time to rebuild. Whyfull helps you decide what to keep.

Where does the report go?

Whyfull runs locally. You choose what to share with your agent; reports can include private paths.

Fair questions.

Why source available, not open source?

Whyfull’s source is public so you can see exactly what it does with your disk, and audit it for security and privacy. It isn’t open source: you may read and review it, not copy or reuse it. Earlier releases stay under Apache-2.0, as published.

Can’t I just build it without the reminder?

You could. The app never locks features; after the trial it shows a reminder at most once a day. The terms still ask for a license to keep using the desktop app. If it saves you space, $19 keeps it going.

Does it phone home?

No. The app and CLI send nothing anywhere: no telemetry, no license check-ins. Keys are verified offline. Buying stores only your email, an optional name, and order details.

Buying for a team or company?

Buy one license per person who uses the desktop app; each covers all of that person’s computers. The CLI is free for everyone, including at work. For volume pricing, use the contact on the terms page.

What if it isn’t for me?

Ask for a refund within 30 days, no questions asked. And if the command line is all you need, it stays free.

What about future versions?

Updates are included. If a future major version is ever a paid upgrade, we’ll say so in advance, and your license keeps working with the versions it covers.
